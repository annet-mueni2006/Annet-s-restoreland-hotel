About David

David Otieno is the visionary behind Restoreland's award-winning spa and wellness programme. A former Olympic massage therapist, David spent years caring for elite athletes before turning his attention to holistic hospitality wellness.

Trained in traditional African healing practices, Japanese shiatsu, and Swedish massage, David brings a rare blend of clinical precision and spiritual depth to his work. He has designed every treatment on our spa menu, including the signature "Savannah Calm" ritual, which uses locally sourced botanicals and warm stone therapy.

Since joining Restoreland in 2019, David has earned our spa the title of "Best Hotel Spa in East Africa" and has built a team of therapists trained in his unique "Land to Body" philosophy — a belief that true healing comes from reconnecting with the earth.

David is also a passionate advocate for sustainable wellness and sources all spa products from Kenyan women-led cooperatives.
