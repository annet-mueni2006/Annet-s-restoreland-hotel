About James

James Mwangi has spent more than 18 years shaping luxury hospitality across East Africa. He began his career as a front desk agent at a small Nairobi hotel, where his gift for making guests feel at home quickly set him apart.

Over the years, he worked his way through operations, guest relations, and eventually senior management, earning a reputation for warmth, precision, and an unwavering commitment to service excellence.

When he joined Restoreland in 2015, James brought with him a simple but powerful philosophy: "Every guest should feel like they've come home." That philosophy now underpins everything we do.
