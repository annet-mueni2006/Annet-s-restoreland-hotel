About Grace

Grace Wanjiru is the heart of Restoreland's guest experience. With a natural gift for reading people and an extraordinary memory for names, preferences, and stories, she has turned hospitality into an art form.

Grace joined Restoreland in 2018 after nearly a decade in luxury travel and concierge services across Dubai and Cape Town. She speaks five languages fluently — English, Swahili, French, Spanish, and Italian — and has a knack for making every guest feel like the only guest.

Famous among returning visitors for remembering their favourite tea, their children's names, and even their preferred pillow firmness, Grace embodies the personal touch that sets Restoreland apart. She once turned a rainy afternoon into a private wine tasting that a guest still calls "the best day of our trip."

Outside the hotel, Grace volunteers with tourism schools, mentoring young women entering the hospitality industry.
