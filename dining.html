Meet Our Executive Chef

Chef Amina Yusuf

Michelin-trained • 20+ years experience

With a passion for blending African heritage with French technique, Chef Amina leads our culinary team with creativity and precision. Her philosophy is simple: respect the ingredient, honour the guest, and never stop innovating.

"Every plate tells a story — of the land, the season, and the people who grew it."
