About Amina

Chef Amina Yusuf is the creative force behind Restoreland's award-winning kitchens. Born in Mombasa and trained at Le Cordon Bleu in Paris, she has spent over 15 years mastering the delicate art of blending East African coastal flavours with classical French technique.

Her journey began in her grandmother's kitchen in Zanzibar, where she learned that the best meals are made with patience, fresh ingredients, and a generous heart. That lesson still guides every dish she creates today.

Since joining Restoreland in 2017, Amina has redefined our culinary identity — introducing farm-to-table menus, partnering with local farmers, and earning our signature restaurant a place among Nairobi's top ten. Her tasting menu, "Swahili Sunrise," is a guest favourite.

When she isn't in the kitchen, Amina mentors young chefs and runs cooking workshops for underprivileged youth in Nairobi.
